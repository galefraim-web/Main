SuperApp Film Production GuideフィジカルAIで、現場の今を経営の判断へ

180 s · six 30 s blocks · 37 shots 16:9 · 4K · 24 fps (60 fps on slow-motion shots) Screens: real SuperApp UI, light mode, added in post

This guide is everything you need to make the film. Work through it from top to bottom: the global section once, then one block at a time. Each block follows the same five steps: new characters, keyframes, the 30-second prompt, post, then a check and a hand-off frame that starts the next block.

The film in one minute

One Tokyo tower, one day, one continuous journey. Arai, the site manager, is the calm still point. A cyan signal thread carries the camera from each scene into the next. Every challenge on site leaves a glowing amber pin on the Living Tower, a digital twin of the building. AI connects the six pins to one cause (the material hoist), Arai approves the plan, and Physical AI acts overnight. The next morning every pin turns cyan, and at dusk the change spreads across the city.

Generation rules (every block)

  1. Characters come first. Build a Soul ID from each character's reference set before generating any shot they appear in. New characters are introduced in the block where they first appear.
  2. Keyframes before video. Every block's keyframes are generated and approved as stills first. They become the start frames, end frames and image references for that block's prompt.
  3. One prompt per block. Each block has a full 30-second multi-shot prompt. If your model caps clip length, use the 3-part split underneath it, chaining the parts with the last frame of each.
  4. Chain the blocks. The last frame of each block is exported as B#-END and used as the first frame of the next. This is what makes the 3 minutes play as one movement.
  5. Never generate our real material. The SuperApp UI, the Trusstor badge close-ups, logos, kanji and on-screen text are added in post. Devices and panels are generated blank with tracking dots.
  6. Negative prompt every time. Until Block 6, also add "robots, autonomous machines" to it.

Style prompts

The block prompts already include these. They're listed here so you can fix a shot or a keyframe on its own.

A

Ultra-real near-future live action

Photorealistic, ultra-detailed cinematic live action, ARRI Alexa 65 large-format look with anamorphic lenses, 24 fps, 4K HDR, subtle film grain, natural skin pores and fabric texture, realistic physics, motion blur and lens behaviour. Setting: an immaculate, orderly 30-storey high-rise construction site in central Tokyo in the near future: white perimeter panels, green scaffold mesh sheets, white tower cranes, a glass-walled site office, clean exposed concrete slabs. Japanese workers in authentic site workwear (white or yellow helmets with chin straps, full-body harnesses, tobi trousers, long-sleeve work jackets), each wearing a small sleek black smart badge on the harness strap with a soft cyan status light. Premium product-film composition, calm, confident, awe-inspiring and real.
B

Cyan connectivity layer

A luminous cyan connectivity layer integrated into the real scene: thin glowing cyan lines with a soft bloom, small light particles travelling along them, gentle pulse rings expanding from smart badges, glowing contour outlines on machines and work zones. The light is depth-correct, passing behind and in front of objects and reflecting faintly on wet concrete, glass and helmets. Elegant, sparse, high-end technology product film. No sci-fi HUD clutter, no text, no numbers.
C

Device screen, light mode

The device screen glows bright white in light mode: a blank pale-grey interface area with four small dark tracking dots in its corners, no readable text, ready for screen replacement. Cool white screen light falls on the face and hands, with natural reflections on the glass.
D

Floating light-mode glass panels

Thin frosted-glass interface panels float in the air in front of the character in light mode: bright white surfaces with soft drop shadows and a fine glowing cyan edge, blank content areas with four small tracking dots for screen replacement. The panels unfold smoothly from the device like folding paper, with realistic depth of field, parallax and reflections. No readable text.
E

The Living Tower (AI illustration world)

AI illustration world: a 30-storey tower rendered as a luminous digital twin of clear glass and fine cyan linework, floating in deep navy-black space. Floor slabs are thin glowing planes, people and machines are tiny particles of light, with soft volumetric glow, isometric precision and cinematic shallow depth of field. Amber glowing pins mark open issues and cyan pins mark resolved ones. Premium, calm, awe-inspiring. No text.
NEG

Negative prompt

cartoon, anime, video game look, plastic CGI look, cluttered sci-fi HUD, neon cyberpunk, purple or pink glow, plain hard cuts, slideshow transitions, distorted faces, extra or missing fingers, changing character face or clothing, missing helmet, worker at height without harness, messy or dirty site, dark-mode user interface, generated user interface, readable generated text, gibberish kanji, logos, watermarks, burned-in subtitles, oversaturated colors, excessive lens flare, stock footage look, robots, autonomous machines

Remove "robots, autonomous machines" only for Block 6.

STEP 3The 30-second Block 1 prompt

Attach: @Arai, @Mori, @Badge, @Glow (KF-04), @Panel (KF-06). Start frame: KF-01. End frame: KF-06, if your model supports one. 16:9, 24 fps, negative prompt in its own field.

A single continuous 30-second cinematic product film in six connected shots, each flowing into the next through a camera move or a match cut, never a plain cut. Photorealistic, ultra-detailed, ARRI Alexa 65 large-format anamorphic look, 24 fps, natural skin and fabric texture, realistic physics. An immaculate near-future high-rise construction site in central Tokyo: a 30-storey concrete tower, white tower cranes, green scaffold mesh, white perimeter panels, a glass-walled site office. Japanese workers in authentic workwear, each with a small sleek black smart badge like @Badge on the harness strap with a soft cyan light. Lead character: site manager @Arai (white helmet, light-grey work jacket, thin dark glasses), calm and composed, the same face in every shot. Foreman @Mori (yellow helmet, harness, radio). Cyan light in the exact style and intensity of @Glow: elegant, depth-correct, soft bloom, never a HUD.

[0–6s] Pre-dawn blue hour. One continuous drone glide from high over the Tokyo skyline toward the tower under construction, its cranes' red aviation lights blinking and site floodlights switching on floor by floor as the camera approaches. Tokyo Skytree softly lit in the distance, thin mist. The glide slows over the open top floor and finds a lone figure in a white helmet at the slab edge, seen from behind: @Arai.

[6–12s] Re-framed on the open concrete floor of Level 7, locked-off and symmetrical: @Arai stands perfectly still in the centre in real time while everything around him races in time-lapse from morning to evening. Crews arrive and relocate, a scissor lift crosses, drywall pallets appear and vanish, barrier tape goes up and comes down, sunlight sweeps across the slab. A printed paper schedule taped to the column beside him flutters but never changes. Then the light snaps back to early morning and everything freezes.

[12–15s] Slow push-in to a close-up of @Arai on the frozen floor. He glances at the paper schedule, then down through the open slab edge toward the ground far below, where workers are gathering in rows. A quiet exhale. The camera tilts down past the slab edge, following his gaze, and drops vertically down the facade.

[15–21s] The drop lands at ground level at 06:30: forty workers in neat rows doing synchronized morning stretches, @Mori leading at the front. A speed ramp slows them to near stillness, arms raised. One by one each worker's badge lights soft cyan, and gentle cyan pulse rings expand from every badge across the crowd like ripples on still water. Glowing cyan contour lines trace the tower cranes, the concrete pump and the material hoist; thin lines draw the work zones on the ground. The camera pushes into an extreme macro of one badge exactly like @Badge as it pulses cyan once.

[21–26s] The pulse breaks into small particles of light and the camera rises fast with thousands of them streaming up the tower facade. As they climb, glowing cyan lines trace the building's columns, slabs and hoist shaft floor by floor, until the whole real tower is wrapped in its own luminous outline against the sunrise. Near the top the streams arc away and dive down toward the glass site office at the base.

[26–30s] The streams pass through the office glass and land on the tablet held by @Arai. Its screen lights bright white and a large frosted light-mode glass panel like @Panel unfolds smoothly into the air above it, with a fine cyan edge and a blank pale content area, no text. Cool white light on his face. The camera pushes slowly past his shoulder toward the centre of the floating panel.

Sound: one continuous cinematic score beginning with low strings and a taiko heartbeat, city hum and a distant crane horn; the morning calisthenics piano and counting "ichi, ni, san, shi" melt into a soft rising shimmer at 15s, with a clean tick for each pulse ring; the music lifts as the tower lights up and settles into an expectant held chord at the panel.

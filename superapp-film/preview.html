How to run the preview

  1. Optional, about 10 minutes: generate the storyboard contact sheet below as a single image and approve the order and the look before any video is made.
  2. Use the keyframes you already have as start frames (the source shot in v2 is listed on each beat). Generate each beat as a 5 s draft clip at 1080p in Higgsfield's faster model, one take only, then trim to the beat's length.
  3. Chain it: where a hand-off says so, use the last frame of one clip as the start frame of the next. That is the flow you're testing.
  4. Comp the 4 real assets roughly: a simple corner-pin in CapCut or Resolve is enough at this stage. No polish.
  5. Put it on a temp music track with the three drops at 0:17, 0:22.5 and 0:26, and record the VO yourself or with TTS.

Sign-off checklist

What to judge on the preview (with Omri) before the high-resolution run:

  • Flow: does it play as one continuous movement, with no beat that feels like a jump? Watch the hand-offs P2→P3, P5→P6 and P8→P9.
  • Glow level: is the cyan elegant and premium (P2, P6, P7), or too sci-fi? Adjust Style B before the full run.
  • Ours: are the badge, the two screens and the logo clearly recognizable, and do the light-mode panels read well?
  • Story: does a first-time viewer get "problems → one cause → action" from P7, P8, P9 and P10 alone?
  • Physical AI: are the robots in P10 credible, and is a "next" label needed?
  • Cast: does Arai's Soul ID hold across P1, P5, P9 and P11?
  • Tone: does 「ヨシ!」 land as sincere and quietly proud rather than comic?

Once it's approved, every beat maps one-to-one to its v2 shot, so the preview's prompts, keyframes and hand-offs carry straight into the 3-minute film.

Prompt (full 30 s, multi-shot)

A single continuous 30-second cinematic product film in 11 connected shots, each flowing into the next through a camera move or a match cut, never a plain cut. Photorealistic, ultra-detailed, ARRI Alexa large-format anamorphic look, 24 fps. A near-future Tokyo high-rise construction site: immaculate, orderly, white tower cranes, green scaffold mesh. Japanese workers in authentic workwear, each wearing a small smart badge like @Badge that glows soft cyan. Throughout, elegant glowing cyan connectivity lines with soft bloom and travelling light particles, depth-correct, premium technology look in the style of @Glow. Lead character: the site manager @Arai (white helmet, light-grey work jacket, thin dark glasses), calm and composed, the same face in every shot.

[0–3.5s] Locked-off symmetrical wide shot of an open concrete floor. @Arai stands perfectly still in the centre in real time while everything around him races in time-lapse: crews relocate, a scissor lift crosses, pallets appear and vanish, sunlight sweeps from morning to evening. The light snaps back to dawn and everything freezes.

[3.5–6s] Match cut down to the ground: a morning assembly of forty workers in rows, frozen mid-stretch with arms raised. Every badge lights cyan and gentle cyan pulse rings ripple across the crowd like water; glowing cyan outlines trace the tower cranes and the material hoist. Slow push-in.

[6–7s] Push into an extreme macro of one badge on a harness strap, exactly like @Badge, pulsing cyan once.

[7–9.5s] The pulse breaks into light particles; the camera rises fast with thousands of them up the tower facade as glowing cyan lines trace every column and slab until the whole tower is wrapped in its own luminous outline, then the particles dive toward a glass site office.

[9.5–12.5s] Inside the glass site office at dawn, @Arai holds a tablet as the particles land on it; a large frosted white light-mode glass panel like @Panel unfolds into the air above it, with a fine cyan edge and a blank pale content area. Cool white light on his face. The camera pushes through the panel.

[12.5–15s] Out to the exterior: a high external scaffold with green mesh sheets billowing in strong wind, workers clipped to the rail. The wind itself becomes visible as translucent cyan streamlines swirling around the tower corner; one streamline flares amber. Drone orbit.

[15–17s] The streamlines sweep down into a top-down view of a concrete slab: time accelerates and each worker leaves a glowing cyan light trail, drawing looping paths and a dense bright knot at the closed hoist doors where pallets wait. The knot turns amber.

[17–21s] Fast pull-back into the digital twin @Twin: the tower as glass and cyan linework floating in navy-black space at night, six amber glowing pins on different floors. Cyan threads connect the pins one by one and converge on the hoist shaft, which ignites into a single brilliant column of light running the full height of the tower.

[21–23.5s] Extreme close-up: @Arai's finger hovers in front of a floating white light-mode glass panel beside the glowing shaft, pauses for one breath and taps; a cyan ripple spreads from his fingertip. Rack focus to his calm face.

[23.5–26s] The ripple races into the night yard: a matte-white autonomous pallet mover with a cyan light strip glides wrapped drywall into the open hoist, whose cyan ring lights as the doors close; then a compact white layout robot draws cyan-lit lines across a clean slab. Wet concrete, moonlight, cyan reflections.

[26–30s] A sunrise flare carries into dusk on the top floor: @Arai raises his arm and points firmly across the site in the Japanese pointing-and-calling gesture. The drone pulls back and rises: the tower glows in calm cyan, and cyan threads spread across the Tokyo skyline to other construction sites, which light up one by one toward Tokyo Skytree and Mt. Fuji in an orange-violet sky. In the final second the city's light contracts into a single thin cyan line on a dark frame.

Sound: one continuous cinematic score: low strings and a taiko heartbeat building, silence at 17s then a rising swell, a soft tap at 22.5s then full drive, a beat of silence at 26s for a calm male voice saying "Yoshi!", then a final orchestral hit with taiko and koto.
